# 风格配方 Style Guide

签名风格「法式漫画影线 / BD Ink Hatching」：法比漫画（bande dessinée）式手绘墨线 + 松弛排线/交叉影线 + 孔版印刷颗粒——亮、透、带手绘的呼吸感。

## 配方 0（签名款，默认）

英文配方句，接在照片内容描述之后：

```
Franco-Belgian bande dessinée style illustration,
hand-drawn black ink outlines with expressive sketchy pen hatching
and cross-hatching, risograph print grain on warm cream paper,
vivid palette of chartreuse yellow-green, teal blue-green shadows,
orange ochre and brick red accents, blue-grey stone and pavement,
off-white paper sky, bright sunny contrast, graphic novel cover feel,
flat colors enriched with hatched shading,
subtle print noise and aged paper speckles, lively editorial charm,
no photorealism, no gradients
```

收尾约束（必加）：

```
everything perfectly still, a frozen quiet moment, no motion blur, upright composition,
same orientation and framing as the reference photo,
vertical composition with margin around the subject
```

铁律：
- **色板**：鲜亮的黄绿（chartreuse）做植物主色，阴影一律用青蓝绿（teal）而不用黑灰，暖橙/赭石/砖红做小面积点缀，地面偏蓝灰，天空就是米白纸本色；整体比上一版「暖调颗粒复古」更亮、对比更明快。
- **质感**：黑色手绘勾线 + 看得见的排线/交叉影线（树叶、树干、阴影处尤其明显），叠加孔版印刷颗粒和旧纸斑点；不是光滑矢量块，也不是水彩晕染。
- **造型**：平面色块 + 影线塑造体积，人物保留手绘漫画感（简洁但可带点面部结构），别写实。
- **光感**：明亮晴天的高反差，明暗靠「暖黄亮部 vs 青蓝暗部」的冷暖对比区分。
- 构图取景跟原照片走，主体四周留余量。

## 变体（用户点名才用）

- **暖调颗粒复古**（上一版签名款）：芥末黄/橄榄绿/天空蓝/橘砖红，干画笔水粉颗粒，暖而稳。配方句 `mid-century retro print illustration with grainy gouache texture, warm cream paper background, cozy palette of mustard yellow, olive green, soft sky blue, brick orange and small ink-black accents, clean black ink outlines, flat gouache color blocks with visible dry-brush grain and subtle paper tooth, rounded friendly shapes, nostalgic storybook charm, warm afternoon light feel, muted but bright, no heavy shadows, no gradients, no photorealism`。
- **浓郁复古**（v1 签名款）：砖红/芥末黄/孔雀蓝重饱和、网点凸版颗粒，用户说"要最浓的"时用。配方句 `mid-century retro print illustration, limited palette of brick red, mustard yellow, teal blue and ink black, halftone dots and letterpress grain, slightly faded vintage ink`。
- **水彩小清新**（已弃用的默认，保留备用）：淡彩晕染、大量留白、浅蓝薄荷粉。配方句 `fresh retro illustration with light watercolor touch, airy cream-white background, pale sky blue mint lemon blush palette, loose ink outlines`。
- **蓝白淡彩**：钴蓝单色+大量留白+松弛墨线，适合厨房、静物。配方句 `loose ink sketch with cobalt blue watercolor washes, mostly white paper, airy and minimal`。
- **美式复古漫画**：粗黑轮廓、半调网点阴影、做旧纸张。配方句 `vintage American comic-book illustration, bold ink outlines, halftone shading, faded newsprint texture`。

默认一律用配方 0；用户说"换种风格"再给变体选项。
